BEFORE YOU GO

A little birthday note

✳

Happy birthday, my beautiful girl. ❤️

I genuinely don't know where to start, because there's so much I want to tell you.

I love you. And I mean it when I say I'm seriously, seriously in love with you. You're not just my girlfriend, you're my best friend, one of my favourite people and someone I genuinely cannot imagine my life without.

It's funny because when you came into my life, I didn't even know how much I needed you. After everything that had happened before, I remember questioning how I could have been so stupid. But you made me realise that you're supposed to be a little stupid in love — it's just that the other person needs to be the right one. And you are.

I don't think there was ever one particular moment when I fell in love with you. I just keep falling more with every second that passes. Like you say, more than yesterday and less than tomorrow.

You brought so much peace into my life. You made me happier, more comfortable and more confident in myself. I can be weird, goofy and completely unfiltered around you. I don't feel like I need to pretend to be someone else. You make me feel appreciated, you remind me that I'm smart, and you make me recognise things about myself that I sometimes forget. I hope you know how much that means to me.

And I hope you can see yourself the way I see you. Because genuinely, you're sublime. You're kind and scary, sweet and bossy, and sometimes an absolute menace, but I wouldn't have it any other way. I wish you could look at yourself through my eyes, even for a moment, and realise just how incredible you are.

I also hope you never underestimate yourself. I know what you're capable of. I've seen you prove yourself, and I genuinely believe that when you put your mind to something, you can achieve it. I want to see you get that A in AS Maths and that A or A* at A-level. I'll be ridiculously proud of you, regardless, but I hope you start believing in yourself as much as I believe in you. Never think you're not smart enough, because you absolutely are.

I hope this next year brings you everything you deserve. I hope in the future, you make loads of money, have all the freedom to do whatever you want, achieve everything you've been working towards and get to experience all the things you dream about, irrelevant of my presence in your life. I still want you to be the happiest.

And I want you to know how much I treasure our little moments. From the hours of talking, to hearing the same wedding drama for the 50th time, to our college breaks, to that Sunday at the park when we had our first hug. I still remember going home that day and being completely unable to think about anything else, or even breathe. And look at us now.

I absolutely love seeing you when you're comfortable around me. When you're giggling, smiling, being all bubbly and letting that guard down. You look like a little baby, and I genuinely feel so proud knowing that you feel safe enough with me to be that way. That smile of yours is worth everything.

And obviously, I haven't forgotten our future. I want our own home, our family, our kids, and all the ordinary little things in between. I want the back hugs while you're doing something, the little kisses, the random conversations, the silly arguments and just getting to live life together. I want all of it with you.

I love you so, so much, Maira. Thank you for being you, for making me happier, for appreciating me, for believing in me and for bringing so much good into my life.

I hope I get to make every birthday of yours special, and I hope this little corner of the internet reminds you just how much you mean to me.

More than yesterday, less than tomorrow.

Happy birthday, meri jaan. ♡
